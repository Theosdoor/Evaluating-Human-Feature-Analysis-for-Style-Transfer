\subsection{Human Patch Extraction}

We use \texttt{YOLO26m} \cite{yolo26_ultralytics} to detect and extract 4000 human patches from the training videos: 2000 for each domain (game/movie). 
Longer videos should be more diverse, so this budget is split proportional to length, so longer videos represent more of the dataset. 
To prioritise diverse patches, we reject frames with inter-frame mean absolute difference below \(\tau_{sc}=8\) (near-static shots), and enforce a minimum spacing of 10 frames between YOLO calls.
Candidate patches are scored by a weighted combination of detection confidence, relative bounding-box area and Laplacian sharpness (Table~\ref{tab:score_weights}). Greedy diversity sampling then enforces a 10-frame temporal gap per video source. Hyperparameters were empirically chosen.

  \centering
  \caption{Composite patch quality score components. Maximum achievable score is 0.75; sharpness is normalised per-source (film vs.\ game) against a blur threshold $\tau$.}

    \toprule
    Component & Formula & Weight \\
    \midrule

    Relative size & soft peak at 15\% of frame area & 0.25 \\
    Laplacian sharpness & $\min(\text{blur}\,/\,3\tau,\;1)$ & 0.20 \\

We classify patches using a 3-layer Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) \cite{kipf2017semisupervisedclassificationgraphconvolutional, Yan_Xiong_Lin_2018} on COCO \cite{lin2015microsoft} skeleton graphs with node features $(x, y, \text{confidence})$, and  confidence-weighted global pooling \cite{confidenceweightedpooling}.
We use \texttt{YOLO26m-pose} \cite{yolo26_ultralytics} to detect pose keypoints for the skeletons.
Table \ref{tab:gcn_results} shows the results.

\begin{table}[h]

  \caption{Rule-based heuristics used to seed manual annotation, applied sequentially.
           Thresholds: $\tau_f{=}0.30$ (face), $\tau_n{=}0.25$ (nose-back), $\tau_b{=}0.10$ (eye-back), $\tau_{\text{bd}}{=}0.20$ (body).}
  \label{tab:rules}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    Step & Condition & Outcome \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{3}{*}{Orientation}
      & nose, l\_eye, r\_eye $\geq \tau_f$                              & front \\
      & nose $< \tau_n$, eyes visible (conf $\geq \tau_b$) $\leq 1$    & back  \\
      & otherwise                                                        & \textit{others} \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{3}{*}{Extent}
      & ankles $\geq 1$ or $\geq 3$ lower kps (conf $\geq \tau_{\text{bd}}$), $h/w \geq 1.5$ & full-body \\
      & $\geq 1$ shoulder visible (conf $\geq \tau_{\text{bd}}$)                               & head-shoulder \\
      & otherwise                                                                               & \textit{others} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

GCN training labels are manually annotated using a custom tool (Figure \ref{fig:annot_tool}) seeded with rule-based keypoint heuristics (Table \ref{tab:rules}).
Horizontal flip augmentation (swapping symmetric keypoint pairs) doubles the training set without requiring additional annotations.
